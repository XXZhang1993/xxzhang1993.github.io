About me

I am a Lecturer (Education and Research) in Cyber Security at the University of Exeter, where I study how to make effective security decisions under uncertainty.

My research focuses on cybersecurity risk management and decision support. I combine attack graphs, game theory, and optimisation to assess attacker–defender interactions under uncertainty and resource constraints. I am a member of Exeter’s Security and Trust of Advanced Systems group.

I joined Exeter in October 2023, following a postdoctoral appointment at Queen Mary University of London. I completed my PhD at Imperial College London in 2019. View my academic background ↓

Prospective students. I welcome enquiries about research in game theory for cybersecurity, risk management, AI for security, and AI safety. Please email me with your CV and a research proposal. See also Exeter–CSC scholarships for 2027 entry.

Research interests

01

Security games and decision support

Modelling the interaction between attackers and defenders, from multistage attacks to adaptive defence. I use attack graphs, Markov models, and game theory to study how limited security budgets can be allocated effectively.

Risk and uncertainty

Developing robust security strategies when attack probabilities, attacker states, and the effectiveness of controls are uncertain. My work examines how defence choices perform across plausible scenarios and changing assumptions.

AI security and trustworthy decisions

Evaluating audio deepfake detection under realistic communication conditions, studying the reliability of LLMs in cybersecurity decision-making, and investigating the risks and governance of organisational AI use.

Research funding

03

Shadow AI in UK Public Sectors: Incentives, Risks, and Governance for Cybersecurity Resilience

Project Lead · British Academy Small Research Grant · Oct 2026–Sep 2028

The project investigates incentives for unauthorised AI use and its cybersecurity risks in UK public sectors, informing governance that balances productivity, compliance, and security. I lead this work with Dr Yuxi Heluo from Exeter Business School.
